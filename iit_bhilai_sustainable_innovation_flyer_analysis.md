# Executive Summary

The flyer promotes a residential certification program by Indian Institute of Technology (IIT) Bhilai titled **“Certification in Foundations of Sustainable Innovation.”** The program is designed for students who have recently passed Class 12 and aims to build practical problem-solving, sustainability awareness, logical thinking, innovation capability, and technical foundations before college.

The program combines:

- Sustainability education
- Programming and computational thinking
- Mechatronics and electronics exposure
- Hands-on learning
- Real-world problem solving
- Mindfulness and purpose-driven thinking
- Communication and presentation skills

The flyer also includes a separate scholarship opportunity targeted toward economically weaker students from the Chhattisgarh (CG) Board.
