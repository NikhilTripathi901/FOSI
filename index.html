About the Programme

Bridging School and
Future Innovation

Bridge to College

Designed for Class 12 passouts to build a strong foundation in logic, programming, and innovation before college.

4-Week Residential

An immersive 4-week offline program at IIT Bhilai campus, including hostel stay, nutritious meals, and peer collaboration.

Learn from Nature

Hands-on experimentation with organic farming, water harvesting, and renewable energy in our dedicated Nature Lab.

Interdisciplinary

Explore the intersection of Python programming, mechatronics, and communication skills for a holistic edge.

Eligibility Criteria

Is This Program
Right For You?

We are looking for motivated students who want to build a sustainable future and bridge the gap between school and college.

  • ✓
    Batch of 2026: Students who have successfully passed their Class 12 board examinations in 2026.
  • ✓
    All Boards Welcome: The program is open to Class 12 passouts from all boards (CBSE, ICSE, CG Board, etc.).
  • ✓
    Scholarship Support: Exclusive scholarship support (via CSR funds) is reserved for students from the Chhattisgarh (CG) Board.
  • ✓
    Academic Merit: A minimum of 65% marks in the board examinations is mandatory for enrollment.
Enrollment Guide

How to Apply & Enroll

Follow these simple steps to secure your spot in the Foundations of Sustainable Innovation program.

01

Online Application

Fill out the official application form with your personal details and Class 12 board results.

Open Form →
02

Document Verification

Upload scanned copies of your Class 12 marksheet and board certificates for eligibility check.

03

Scholarship Request

CG Board students can apply for the CSR scholarship by providing a family income certificate.

Note: If you don't have an income certificate right now, you can submit an Affidavit stating that you will provide the certificate soon.
04

Final Selection

Selected candidates will receive a confirmation email with further instructions for the residential stay.
